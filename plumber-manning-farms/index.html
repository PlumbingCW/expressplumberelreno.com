What Manning Farms is

Manning Farms is a residential community in El Reno, Oklahoma, ZIP 73036, built by CraftHomes. A community listing places it on North Manning Road at the corner of Britton Road, and homes there carry Manning Farms Boulevard addresses.

Larger lots

What more land means for plumbing

On a larger lot, pipes can run farther. The line from the street or the well to the house is longer, there may be more outdoor faucets, and sprinkler or livestock lines sometimes get added later. A longer run means more places for a freeze or a root to cause trouble.

A yard patch that stays wet in dry weather, or a water meter that keeps moving with everything off, points toward an outdoor leak. Leak detection and water line repair cover that kind of problem.

Checklist

A short plumbing checklist for a larger lot

  • ✓Know where the main shutoff is and test it once a year.
  • ✓Disconnect hoses and drain outdoor faucets before a freeze.
  • ✓Note where any cleanouts, tanks or covers sit so you can find them quickly.
  • ✓Keep drains free of wipes, grease and anything that is not toilet paper.
  • ✓Check the meter with every fixture off if your bill jumps.
