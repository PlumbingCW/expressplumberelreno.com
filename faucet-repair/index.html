Check these before anything else

A small puddle under a sink is easy to ignore, and expensive when ignored.

  • 1Close the shutoff valves on the hot and cold supply lines.
  • 2Empty the cabinet and dry it with towels.
  • 3Run the water and watch where the first drop appears.
  • 4Call the 24/7 line if the leak is at a valve, a pipe joint or a supply hose.
Hard water

Why faucets clog and wear

Minerals in the water collect on aerators and inside cartridges and cut the flow. If you see white crusting, test your water. The City of El Reno can share its water quality report, and a hardness test strip is inexpensive.

A water softener protects faucets and fixtures. Until then, unscrewing the aerator and soaking it in vinegar restores flow.

New faucets sold in the United States must meet federal flow limits, and a faucet carrying the EPA WaterSense label meets a stricter efficiency standard, which the International Plumbing Code (IPC) also references for flow rates.

Garbage disposals

Kitchen sinks come with extra parts

A kitchen sink often has a disposal and a dishwasher connection under it. Leaks there are different from a faucet leak. See garbage disposal repair if the unit is humming, jammed or leaking from the bottom.

Faucet types

Know what kind of faucet you have

Most household faucets fall into four types. Compression faucets have separate hot and cold handles and use rubber washers that wear out. Cartridge and ball faucets have a single handle and a sealed insert that is replaced as one part. Ceramic disc faucets are smooth and long lived, but a cracked disc means a new cartridge.

Knowing the type helps the plumber bring the right part. Take a photo of the faucet and any brand name stamped on the base or handle, and send the details when you call. Finding a cartridge for a discontinued model is often what pushes a repair into replacement.

Maintenance

Small habits that keep faucets working

  • ✓Unscrew and soak aerators in vinegar every few months if your water is hard.
  • ✓Turn handles gently. Over-tightening wears seats and washers.
  • ✓Exercise the shutoff valves under sinks once a year so they do not seize.
  • ✓Replace braided supply lines every several years, since they fail without warning.
  • ✓Wipe fixtures dry after use if you see mineral spots forming.
