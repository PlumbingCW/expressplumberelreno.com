Choose a service

Pick the page that matches your problem. Each one explains the signs, the options and what affects the price. If you are unsure, call and describe what you see.

Water Heater Replacement

A tank that leaks from the body cannot be patched. We swap gas and electric units and take the old one away.

Water Heater Repair

Pilot out, cold showers or a valve that drips. We find the failed part before quoting a repair.

Drain Cleaning

A sink, tub or toilet that will not drain gets a cable, a jetter or a camera, depending on the clog.

Water Heater Installation

Planning a new build, a remodel or a move to the garage? We handle venting, relief piping and permits.

Tankless Water Heaters

On-demand hot water needs the right gas line and vent. We install, descale and repair these units.

Hydro Jetting

When a snake only buys a few weeks, high-pressure water scours the pipe wall clean.

Leak Detection

A moving meter or a warm floor means water is escaping. We locate it before anyone cuts into a wall.

Sewer Line Repair

Roots, cracks and sags in the main line are fixed by spot repair, lining or replacement.

Sewer Camera Inspection

A video of the inside of your sewer line, useful before a purchase or after repeat backups.

Sewer Line Cleaning

When every drain in the house backs up, the main line needs clearing from a cleanout.

Commercial Plumbing

Kitchens, restrooms and water heaters in shops and offices, with after-hours service when it counts.

Toilet Repair and Installation

Running, rocking or overflowing toilets get new parts, a reset seal or a full replacement.

Faucet and Sink Repair

Worn cartridges, stuck shutoff valves and under-sink leaks are fixed or the fixture is replaced.

Garbage Disposal

If the unit hums and will not spin, leaks or smells, we repair it or fit a new one.

Repiping

Brown water and repeat leaks in aging pipe are solved by replacing the supply lines.

Plumbing Installation

Remodels and additions need lines roughed in correctly before the walls close.

Slab Leak Repair

Leaks in lines under the concrete are pinpointed, then spot repaired or rerouted overhead.

Water Softener and Filtration

Test your water first, then choose the softener or filter that actually fits the problem.

Burst Pipe Repair

A split pipe from a freeze or high pressure is stopped, repaired and tested.

Water Line Repair

A soggy yard or sudden low pressure often traces to the pipe between the meter and the house.

Backflow Testing

Irrigation systems, hose bibs and commercial lines depend on devices that must be tested.

Grease Trap Cleaning

Kitchens that fry food need traps and lines that are cleared before a busy night.

Cast Iron Pipe Repair

Old drain pipe rusts through. We clamp, splice, line or replace the failing sections.
