When a clogged drain is an emergency

Four things to do before the plumber arrives

Not every clog is urgent. A single slow bathroom sink can wait for a daytime visit. A drain becomes an emergency when wastewater is rising, when several fixtures are affected at once, or when the smell tells you sewage is involved.

  • 1Stop using every sink, tub, shower and toilet that shares the blocked line.
  • 2Turn off the washing machine and dishwasher mid-cycle if they drain into the same pipe.
  • 3Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into standing water. It can burn skin and makes the plumber's work more hazardous.
  • 4Keep children and pets away from any water that came up out of a floor drain or toilet.
Symptoms

How drain clogs show up in El Reno homes

The first sign is rarely a total blockage. Most clogs announce themselves weeks earlier, and recognizing the pattern tells the plumber where to start.

One fixture drains slowly

A single sink or tub with a slow, gurgling drain usually has a hair, soap or grease buildup in its own branch line.

Two or more fixtures slow at once

When the tub and a nearby toilet both struggle, the blockage is past where their branches join.

Washer standpipe overflows

Laundry water backing up through the standpipe or a floor drain points to the main line, not the washer.

Toilet bubbles when a sink drains

Air pushed back through the traps means the main line is partly blocked and close to a full backup.

Kitchen sinks

Kitchen sink clogs have their own causes

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom drains. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe and coats the walls, then catches rice, coffee grounds and food fibers until the opening is the size of a pencil. A disposal grinds food finely but does nothing about the grease that carries it.

If the sink backs up into the other basin of a double sink, the trouble is usually in the shared trap arm or the first few feet of the wall line, which a cable can reach quickly. If water also appears in the dishwasher, the dishwasher drain connects to the same blockage. A stuck or humming disposal is a separate repair, covered on our garbage disposal repair page.

Drain and vent sizing follows the International Plumbing Code (IPC), which El Reno adopts with local amendments, so a plumber who changes a drain layout should be able to point to the section that allows it.

Local housing

Older lines near the Rock District, newer lines on the edges

El Reno was incorporated in 1890, and the blocks around the Rock District and the old Carnegie Library hold some of the city's oldest buildings. Older sewer laterals in neighborhoods like that can be clay or cast iron, and they tend to shed scale and let tree roots in at the joints.

A cable clears the roots, but roots in a joint grow back, which is why a repeat clog in an older house is a reason to schedule a camera look.

The city's own plumbing code also matters here. Chapter 142 of the El Reno code requires a backwater valve on a building sewer that connects to the public sewer. If wastewater came up in the lowest drain of your house during a heavy rain, ask whoever you hire whether a valve is installed and working, because a stuck valve causes backups that look like a clog.

Hidden leaks

When the drain line itself is leaking

Sometimes a drain keeps clogging because the pipe has a belly or a crack where solids settle. A leaking drain line under a slab or in a crawlspace can also leave a damp spot, a musty smell, or a patch of soft flooring that has nothing to do with the supply lines.

A camera run shows whether the pipe is sound. If it is cracked or sagging, the fix moves from cleaning to sewer line repair, and the camera footage gives you something concrete to show an insurance adjuster or a landlord.
