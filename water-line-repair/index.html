What it is

Where the water line begins and ends

The service line starts at the water meter and runs underground to the house. The City of El Reno owns the meter and the main in the street. In general, the property owner is responsible for the pipe on the house side of the meter. Call the City of El Reno at 405-262-4070 to confirm where the boundary is on your lot.

This matters when a line fails, because a leak before the meter is a city repair and a leak after it is yours.

Water service pipe must meet the International Plumbing Code (IPC) and be marked to a recognized standard, and 811 locates are required before excavation under the Oklahoma One-Call system.

Symptoms of a failing service line

Call if you see any of these

One symptom alone is a reason to check. Two together are a reason to call tonight if the yard is flooding.

  • 1A wet or sunken patch in the yard when nothing is irrigating.
  • 2Water pressure that dropped across the whole house.
  • 3A meter that moves with every tap off.
  • 4A sudden jump in your water bill.
  • 5Muddy or discolored water at the taps.

Before digging

Call 811 before anyone digs

Oklahoma requires a call before digging so buried utilities are marked. The Oklahoma One-Call system, reached by dialing 811, is the free way to do that. A plumber handles this as part of an excavation, but if you plan to dig for a fence or a tree, do it yourself first.

The risk is real. A shovel through a gas or electric line is far worse than a water leak, and marking the lines also shows you where the water line lies.

Pressure

Low pressure is not always a leak

A clogged screen, a partly closed valve, a failing pressure regulator or a mineral-choked pipe can all lower pressure. Compare hot and cold water at several fixtures, because if only hot water is weak the water heater is more likely the cause, and if the whole house is weak the service line or the regulator is.

A plumber can test pressure with a gauge at an outdoor spigot, which separates a supply problem from a problem inside the house.
