The area

Central El Reno and its older housing

El Reno was incorporated in 1890 and grew around the railroad, Fort Reno and later Route 66, so its center is much older than the subdivisions on its edges. Downtown includes the Rock District and the Carnegie Library, and older homes sit in the blocks around it.

A neighborhood data profile for the central El Reno tract, which it labels City Center, reports that many homes there were built between 1940 and 1969 and that some are older than 1940. It describes the housing as mostly single-family homes plus apartment complexes. We could not verify exact boundaries for this area, so we use the name Downtown El Reno loosely for the older central blocks.

This page covers homes. If you own a shop or office downtown, see commercial plumbing instead.

Older homes

What age can mean for the plumbing

The points below are general. They describe what is possible in houses from these decades, not what your house has. A camera look or an inspection tells you for sure.

Older drain lines

Drains from the mid-century era may be cast iron or clay. Roots find joints, and old iron can crack or scale inside. A repeat clog may be the pipe, not the hair or grease.

Supply lines that have been replaced, or not

Houses this age may have had their water lines replaced at some point, or still have originals. Low pressure, rusty water or repeat leaks are reasons to ask.

Additions and patched work

Older houses often have remodels layered on top of the original plumbing. Mixed materials and odd routing are common, so expect a plumber to trace the system before cutting.

Sewer laterals

The line from the house to the city sewer can be as old as the house. A camera inspection shows its condition before a small problem becomes a dig.

Before you buy

Buying an older home near downtown

If you are looking at an older house, a sewer camera inspection before closing can show the lateral's condition. A standard home inspection does not always cover the line under the yard, so ask whether it was included.

If the inspection shows damage, sewer line repair options range from spot repairs to replacement, and the right one depends on what the camera finds.
