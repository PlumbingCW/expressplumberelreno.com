First minutes

What to do when a pipe bursts

Stop the water before anything else

Speed limits the damage. These steps are in order.

  • 1Close the main water shutoff, often a wheel or lever where the line enters the house or at the meter box.
  • 2Switch off power to any wet area only if you can reach the panel dry.
  • 3Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines and relieve pressure.
  • 4Move valuables, rugs and electronics out of the water.
  • 5Photograph the damage for insurance, then call the 24/7 line.
Freezes

Why pipes burst in an Oklahoma cold snap

Water expands when it freezes. A frozen section blocks flow, and pressure builds between the ice and the closed faucet until the pipe splits. The split is often found only after the ice thaws.

Oklahoma winters swing. A mild week can be followed by a hard freeze, and the National Weather Service in Norman and the Oklahoma Mesonet publish the forecasts. Before one arrives, drip a faucet on exterior walls, open cabinet doors under sinks, disconnect hoses and insulate lines in garages and crawlspaces.

Repairs should follow the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted in El Reno, and new PEX tubing should be marked to ASTM F876, the standard for cross-linked polyethylene tubing, so ask the plumber what pipe is going in.

Aftermath

Drying out and documenting

Standing water soaks into drywall and flooring within hours. Ask your insurer whether they require a restoration contractor, and keep receipts. Dry the area quickly, open doors and windows if weather allows, and run fans.

Check whether your policy covers sudden pipe bursts, which many do, and ask whether freeze damage carries conditions such as keeping the house heated.
