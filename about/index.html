Who we are

An El Reno plumbing line with a simple promise

El Reno Express Plumbing serves El Reno and Canadian County by phone. We answer a single emergency line around the clock and send a plumber to homes and businesses in El Reno, Yukon, Piedmont and nearby towns. We take calls by phone only, so there is no form to fill out and no account to create.

We keep the promises small and checkable. The line is answered 24/7. A plumber is scheduled the same day when one is available. You hear a clear price before work starts. Beyond that, we tell you what a problem is, what the options are and what affects the cost, which is what each of our service pages sets out.

Licensing

How to check any plumber, including us

Oklahoma licenses plumbers through the Construction Industries Board, and the City of El Reno expects plumbing contractors to be registered. Ask any plumber for a license number and confirm it on the state lookup. We encourage that, and the same goes for permits, which El Reno Community Development handles at 405-262-2000.

What we cover

Services for homes and businesses

Our plumbers handle the problems that cannot wait, such as burst pipes, sewer backups and failed water heaters, and the planned work that prevents them, such as leak detection, repiping and camera inspections. Businesses call us for commercial plumbing, grease control and backflow testing.

We do not pretend to cover trades we do not do. Septic tank pumping, well pump work and roofing are separate trades. When a problem belongs to one of them, we say so and point you to the right kind of contractor.

Straight talk

How we handle price, timing and promises

A plumber cannot responsibly name a price for a hidden problem over the phone, so we do not. You hear a clear price once the plumber has seen the problem, and you approve it before the work starts. Each service page lists the factors that move the price, so you know what to ask about.

On timing, we promise what we can keep. The line is answered 24/7, and same-day appointments are available when a crew is free. We do not promise a fixed arrival window, because traffic, weather and the job before yours can change it. The dispatcher will tell you when a plumber is on the way.

El Reno roots

Why local knowledge matters for plumbing

El Reno was incorporated in 1890, and the city's housing runs from century-old blocks near the Rock District to newer subdivisions on its edges. Older sewer laterals and cast iron stacks behave differently from the PEX and PVC in newer homes, and a plumber who knows the difference diagnoses faster.

Weather matters too. Central Oklahoma can swing from mild days to a hard freeze, and the National Weather Service in Norman issues the warnings that tell you when to protect your pipes.

The city's own rules matter as well. El Reno adopts the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with local amendments, including a backwater valve requirement on building sewers that connect to the public sewer. Our drain cleaning and sewer repair pages explain how that affects backups.

Before you hire anyone

Five checks that work for any plumber

  • ✓Ask for the state license number and confirm it on the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board lookup.
  • ✓Ask whether the work needs a permit and who pulls it.
  • ✓Ask for a clear price before work starts, in writing when the job is large.
  • ✓Ask what the warranty covers and for how long.
  • ✓Keep the invoice and any permit paperwork with your house records.
