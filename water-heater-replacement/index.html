Leak first

A leaking water heater: what to do right now

Five steps while you wait for the plumber

A small drip from a pipe fitting is a repair. Water coming from the bottom or the seam of the tank means the steel has failed, and a tank that has failed cannot be patched.

  • 1Close the cold water shutoff valve above the tank, usually a handle or wheel on the inlet pipe.
  • 2Turn a gas unit's control knob to off or pilot, or switch the electric unit's breaker off.
  • 3Open a hot water tap upstairs to relieve pressure and drain the lines.
  • 4Move boxes and stored items away from the floor around the tank.
  • 5Do not touch the heater or stand in water near it if the electric unit is still on.

How to tell whether the tank is finished

Age, symptoms and the condition of the tank together decide it. A plumber looks at all three rather than just the date on the label.

Lifespan

How long a tank water heater lasts

Conventional tank heaters generally run for roughly a decade, sometimes a few years longer. Check the data plate on the side of your unit. The serial number usually encodes the month and year it was made, and the manufacturer's website or the plumber can decode it.

Water chemistry changes that. Hard water leaves scale inside the tank and on the elements, and the City of El Reno publishes water quality information you can ask for. If you do not know your hardness, a test strip from a hardware store tells you in a minute. A water softener can slow the scale that shortens a heater's life.

Choices

What you can choose when the old tank is replaced

Same-size tank, same fuel

The fastest swap. Gas stays gas, electric stays electric, and the existing connections are reused where they are sound.

Larger tank

A bigger tank helps a growing household, but it must fit the closet and meet venting and clearance rules.

Tankless unit

Heats on demand and takes up little space, but needs a proper gas line or electrical supply. See tankless water heaters.

Fuel change

Switching between gas and electric means new lines or a new circuit, so it takes longer and costs more.

Code and permits

What El Reno code asks of a new heater

El Reno adopts the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with local amendments, and plumbing work by contractors must be done by a state-plumber who is also registered with the city. A new water heater normally needs a temperature and pressure relief valve piped to a safe termination, proper venting for gas units, and an expansion tank where the system is closed.

Ask the plumber whether a permit applies and who pulls it. You can also confirm any plumber's state license on the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board lookup, and call El Reno Community Development at 405-262-2000 about permit rules. Because rules vary, get the answer in writing before the work starts.
